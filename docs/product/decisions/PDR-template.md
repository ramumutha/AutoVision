# PDR-NNN: Decision Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD or Not recorded
- **Owner:** Product owner or decision forum
- **Related features:** Feature IDs

## Context / Problem

Describe the user, dealer, market, or delivery problem and cite supporting research/evidence. Separate facts from hypotheses.

## Decision

State the product decision and explicit exclusions.

## Rationale

Explain why this option best serves the product objective, including relevant evidence confidence and tradeoffs.

## Implications

Record expected user, commercial, data, security, accessibility, operations, roadmap, and demo consequences. Do not imply implementation is complete unless evidence exists.

## Related Sources

Link research records, feature/release registers, functional UX, ADRs, implementation, and tests.

## Review Trigger

State the evidence, date, market change, product outcome, or architecture change that should reopen the decision.
