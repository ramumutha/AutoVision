# Market Opportunities

The repository demonstrates Service Profit product capability, not market size or commercial demand. No defensible TAM/SAM/SOM, competitor comparison, pricing study, or customer adoption data is recorded.

## Evaluation Questions

For each opportunity, assess:

- dealer pain severity and frequency;
- revenue or ROI potential;
- addressable market reach;
- differentiation and defensibility;
- evidence confidence;
- implementation/integration effort;
- data dependency and quality;
- operational, privacy, security, and regulatory risk.

Use the [AutoVision Opportunity Score](../roadmap/product-roadmap.md#autovision-opportunity-score) to structure discussion, not to replace Product Owner judgment.
