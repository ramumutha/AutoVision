# AutoVision Market Strategy

## Evidence Boundary

No external market study, validated customer interview corpus, competitive benchmark, pricing study, or production dealer outcome dataset is currently recorded in this repository. This document therefore separates repository-supported positioning from hypotheses requiring research.

## Supported Positioning

**IMPLEMENTED PRODUCT CAPABILITY:** AutoVision Service Profit helps an authenticated dealership manager review service revenue opportunities across declined work, deferred work, due/overdue service, and inactive-customer signals. It provides evidence, actionability, commercial potential, suppression, and required-review safeguards.

**DEMO EVIDENCE:** The current synthetic R1 dataset is classified `SYNTHETIC_DEMO_ONLY`, uses market `IN` and currency `INR`, and exercises ten scenarios. It proves product behavior and demo readiness; it does not prove market demand or production-data quality.

## Market Hypotheses

The following are **RESEARCH REQUIRED**, not established facts:

- dealerships experience material revenue leakage from incomplete follow-up of declined/deferred work;
- managers will prioritize evidence-backed opportunities over raw campaign lists;
- DMS data can support the required opportunity classes consistently across providers and markets;
- explanation, suppression, and human-review safeguards improve trust and adoption;
- the current workflows generalize beyond the synthetic India demo context.

Research records should identify market, dealer segment, vehicle class/powertrain applicability, source, and confidence. See [research index](../research/research-index.md).

## Segmentation Questions

Before choosing a target segment, validate:

- dealer group versus single-site operating needs;
- franchise, independent, and multi-brand data availability;
- market-specific consent and outreach constraints;
- passenger, commercial, two-wheeler, and EV service differences;
- DMS integration effort and evidence quality;
- decision-maker, daily user, and buyer roles.

## Differentiation Direction

Potential differentiation should be assessed around explainable evidence, DMS-neutral portability, tenant-safe workflows, and measurable service value. These are architectural strengths and product hypotheses; they are not yet externally benchmarked claims.

## Commercial Validation Needed

**RESEARCH REQUIRED:** willingness to pay, packaging, implementation economics, expected recovery rate, time-to-value, retention impact, and support cost. No revenue forecast or ROI target should enter roadmap commitments without an evidence record and Product Owner decision.
